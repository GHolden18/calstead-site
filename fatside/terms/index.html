Fatside is a training game for golf decisions. Its numbers, marked Est., are a model’s estimates for a typical golfer at your level. They aren’t a measure of your game, and Fatside doesn’t promise better scores. It isn’t professional instruction. On a real course, play safely and follow the course’s rules.
