Fatside

The thinking part of golf, without the swing.

Fatside is a daily game about course management: which club, which shot, and where to aim. Every day there is one new hole, and everyone plays the same one, so you can compare how you played it with your friends.

How a hole plays

  1. Build the shot

    Choose a club from the bag, the kind of shot and its shape, then drag to aim. The picture shows where it would land and stop.

  2. Swing

    Pull back for power, let go, then tap to stop the meter. A cleaner strike sends the ball closer to where you planned.

  3. See the better play

    After each shot, Fatside grades the call, says in one line what it cost, and draws the play it rates best.

What is in it

  • A new hole every day. Scored out of 100 on your decisions, with a streak and a card to share.
  • The putt is a read. Greens are colored by slope. You pick the line and the pace.
  • Lessons you play. Tracks on the fundamentals, reading greens, yardage books and different styles of course, each one a hole you play through.
  • Your own bag. Set the clubs you carry and how far each one goes.
  • A weekly recap and your golfer type. The habit that shows most in your calls, with the counts behind it.

What Fatside says, and what it doesn’t

Every number in Fatside is the app’s own estimate, worked out from a model of how shots tend to finish, and the app labels it that way. A page inside the app lists where each number comes from.

Fatside makes no promise about your scores. It is a game about decisions, and it shows you its reasoning.
