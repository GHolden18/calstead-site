Privacy policy

Knavery, by Calstead Studios. Last updated November 26, 2026.

The short version

Knavery has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your games, your history and the Seeker’s notes about you stay on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Three things. Your Odd One Out pick: when you lock in a number, the app sends that number and a random ID made on your phone when you first opened the app. The ID is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. Picks are stored with the date so they can be counted, and only counts per number are ever shown. And your Same Brain words: when you lock in a word, the app sends it with today’s topic and the same random ID, to check the spelling; after each round, it sends the topic and the words you and Echo have said in that game, so Echo can pick its next word. Your words are not stored; the server only counts how many checks and words each random ID asked for that day, to keep usage in check. And what you type in the AI-hosted games (Say Less, Tip of the Tongue and On the Dial): your clues, guesses and the things you name on the dial (with the dial’s two ends) are sent with the date and the same random ID, along with what was already said in that game, so the game’s host can answer. They are not stored either, except that the same count is kept.

Where it goes

Odd One Out picks and the Same Brain counts are stored with Supabase, our database host. To check your spelling, pick Echo’s word and play the other hosts (Blurt, Sandy and Gauge), our server passes what you typed in that game (not your ID) to Anthropic’s Claude model. Like any web request, the servers see your IP address when something is sent; it is not stored with your pick or your words.

Purchases

Purchases are not live yet. When they are, they go through Apple, and RevenueCat will check whether your plan is active. This policy will say so before that happens.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything on your phone. To remove your Odd One Out picks from the server, email founder@calsteadstudios.com with the date you played.
