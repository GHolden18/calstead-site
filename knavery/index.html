Knavery

Seven short daily games. One try each a day.

Knavery is a bundle of short daily games, each played against its own character. The day’s games are the same for everyone, and every result becomes a poster to share that never gives the answer away.

Follow along on TikTok

Knavery is in testing on iPhone. We’ll put the link here when you can try it.

How a day plays

  1. Play against the host

    Each game has its own character and rules you learn in seconds. In Same Brain, you and Echo name a word at the same time and try to land on the same one.

  2. One try a day

    Each game takes a few minutes, and everyone gets the same one. In Nobody Home, the Seeker picks where to look before you hide.

  3. Get your poster

    Every result becomes a poster with your score and a one-line verdict, ready to post. It never shows the answer, so it won’t spoil anyone’s day.

What is in it

  • Same Brain, free every day. Match Echo’s word in as few rounds as you can.
  • A hiding game that learns you. Nobody Home’s Seeker plans his search from where you’ve hidden before, and seals the plan before you hide.
  • A case that never needs a guess. Whodunit’s six suspects are the same for everyone, and every clue they leave is true.
  • Word games with AI hosts. Say Less, Tip of the Tongue and On the Dial answer what you type.
  • A timing test with no help. Dead Stop’s clock goes dark, and you count the rest in your head.

What Knavery says, and what it doesn’t

Knavery doesn’t read minds. The games that seem to know you learn from the choices you’ve made before, and each one makes its call before you choose and shows it afterwards.

There are no prizes and no money to win. Posters and share text never give away the word, the clue, the hiding spot or the culprit.

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The privacy policy says exactly what leaves your phone.
