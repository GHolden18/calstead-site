Fatside

The thinking part of golf, without the swing. Every day there is one new hole, and everyone plays the same one.

  • You pick the club, the shape of the shot and where to aim.
  • Fatside shows how that call tends to play out, and what the better play was.
  • Short lessons you play through cover reading greens, yardage books and different styles of course.

The odds in Fatside are its own estimates, and the app says so. It makes no promise about anyone’s scores.
