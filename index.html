Knavery

Seven short daily games, each played against its own character. The day’s games are the same for everyone.

  • One try at each game a day.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
  • Every result is a poster to share, never a spoiler.

Fatside

The thinking part of golf, without the swing. Every day there is one new hole, and everyone plays the same one.

  • You pick the club, the shape of the shot and where to aim.
  • Fatside shows how that call tends to play out, and what the better play was.
  • Short lessons you play through cover reading greens, yardage books and different styles of course.

About the studio

Calstead Studios LLC was founded in 2026 by Gabe Holden. We design, build and run our own software products, from games to everyday tools, and we take each one from the first idea to the App Store ourselves.

What you do in our products stays on your device wherever it can, and each one’s privacy policy says exactly what leaves it.
